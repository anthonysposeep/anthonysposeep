About

Anthony Sposeep writes about love, grief, memory, faith, and the things people carry long after they should have put them down.

His debut collection, Murder of Your Memory, is forthcoming.

He lives in Texas with his cat, Charlie, who remains largely unimpressed by the whole thing.
